A practice built on the written record

Kernel Co. is a project management consultation firm working with clients internationally, at the point where projects succeed or stall: the movement of information. Drawings, bills of quantities, technical submittals, and review comments are received, tracked, and returned on committed dates — nothing is left to memory.

Alongside its consultation work, the firm designs structured learning programmes through Kernel Learning, bringing the same rigour to curriculum, materials, and delivery.
