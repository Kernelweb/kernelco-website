Oil & Gas client Landfill Extension — 3D Earthworks Model

An interactive WebGL model of the landfill extension earthworks. It layers the natural ground surface from the pre-topographic survey (EL 71.49–73.50 m), the cut and fill areas measured against NGL, the 500 mm gatch base formation layer, the new bundwall on three sides alongside the existing bundwall on the NE side with volumes tabulated per 250 mm level lift, the geosynthetics anchor trench, roads, ramps and platform, and the basal lining system.

The viewer is interactive — orbit the model, adjust the vertical exaggeration, toggle each layer's display, and probe levels at any point on the surface.
